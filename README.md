## Work In Progress

For now this mod adds 8 equipments from the Binding of Isaac

## Equipments

### D4

Reroll all of your passive items into other random passive items

### D6

Reroll every item within 10 meters to another one of the same loot pool

### D8

Roll a 0.5x to 2x multiplier for each of your maxhp, damage, attack speed, and max speed

### Crooked Penny

Either duplicates all items within 10 meters or deletes them and drops one (1) gold

### Diplopia

Duplicate all the items within 10 meters before removing your active item

### Spindown Dice

Rerolls all items within 10 meters by decreasing their internal ID number by one

### Void

Upon use, destroys all pickups within 10 meters. If Void destroys a passive item gain 1 of 4 stats (damage, attack speed, critical chance, horizontal speed, vertical speed). If Void destroys an equipment, it gains it's ability, which will happen everytime after Void's effect

### Portable Slot 

Never stop gambling with less cost and worse odds

## Passive

### A Dollar

Gives 100 gold (scaled by difficulty) when you pick it up

### Mini Mush

Makes you smaller and faster

### 3 Dollar Bill 

25% chance on hit to activate the effect of one of 19 random on hit items, scaling per stack.
